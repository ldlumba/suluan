SULUAN staging pilot — privacy notice

SULUAN is a research repository pilot for the University of Antique Research and Extension Office. This page covers the staging pilot only; it is not the institutional privacy policy, which remains a Stage 9 deliverable.

The staging environment holds no production data — only generated smoke fixtures and papers whose authors have opted into the pilot. Accounts are limited to authorized UA Google sign-ins. Notification emails are sent from the pilot sender mailbox to submitters and REO reviewers as part of the submission workflow. For REO's traditional email-based review, a submitted paper may also be copied into the configured REO mailbox as an attachment when it is within the pilot's email size limit; larger papers remain available only through SULUAN.

Questions about the pilot: contact the Research and Extension Office.
